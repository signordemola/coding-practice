## The AI interview (from OpenTrain's docs)

Source: [OpenTrain docs: AI interview](https://www.opentrain.ai/docs/trainers/ai-interview/) (read 26 Nov 2026).

- It's a **screening step before your proposal**. You can't skip it or ask for another format.
- **Text or voice:** the job decides. Text = a chat inside the proposal window, one question at a time, until every required field is answered. Voice = about 5 minutes, spoken.
- **What it asks:**
  - your LinkedIn URL (paste the **full link**: linkedin.com/in/demolastudio)
  - years of experience in AI training, data labeling or annotation
  - weekly availability in hours
  - previous AI training experience: platforms used and task types (annotation, RLHF, SFT, evaluation)
  - any extra questions the employer added
- **Voice rules:** answer right after each question; **two unanswered questions end the interview**. Stay on the tab (leaving is flagged). You can't pause. "Start over" works once.
- **No retake button** once it's finished. A text interview that gets interrupted can be continued.
- No score is shown. The employer decides; the result shows on My Proposals.
